# UIDAI Home Page Topology

1. Fixed header: top accessibility strip, logo/search row, navigation row.
2. Hero carousel: wide rounded banner with side peeks and pagination.
3. Aadhaar services: lavender panel with four service cards and three supporting cards.
4. Aadhaar on Mobile: QR code and store badges, left aligned with large whitespace on desktop.
5. About UIDAI and Updates: mint panel with image/text column and update tabs/list.
6. Aadhaar dashboard: full-width green metric band.
7. Work at UIDAI: peach image-backed CTA card.
8. Aadhaar ecosystem: three lavender cards.
9. Come Build With Us: light network-background CTA card.
10. Have Doubts: videos and FAQ panel in a lavender container.
11. Footer: light patterned background, link columns, office selector/contact details, copyright strip.
12. Floating chatbot mascot at bottom-right.

Page layout: fixed header overlays flow content; main content starts after header with `padding-top` near 220px on desktop. Main content width is approximately 1196px centered at 1440px viewport. Footer spans full width.
